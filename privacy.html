Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains what Chaney’s Pressure Washing & Soft Wash does with the information you send us through this website. It is written in plain language, and it is short because we collect very little.

What we collect

The only information we collect is what you type into the quote request form:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number and email address
  • The property address or ZIP code you want serviced
  • Whether the property is residential or commercial, and the service you need
  • Any message and photos you choose to attach
  • Your preferred contact method

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not run advertising trackers on this site.

How we use it

We use your details for one purpose: to respond to your request, prepare a quote, and carry out the work if you decide to hire us. We contact you using the method you selected on the form.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone except a service provider that helps us receive and store form submissions.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests for as long as we need them to serve you and to keep ordinary business records. If you would like your details removed, contact us and we will take care of it.

Cookies

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated first.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Just get in touch using the details below.
